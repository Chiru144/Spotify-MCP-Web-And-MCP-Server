Vibe & Mood Recommendations

get_recommendations / POST /api/recommendations
MCP Tool Web Modal POST API

Generates a set of tracks from up to 5 combined seeds (genres, track IDs, artist IDs). Spotify removed its recommendations endpoint and audio features for Development Mode apps, so recommendations are songs by the seed artists' collaborators plus picks from a genre search. Audio targets (energy, valence, tempo) are accepted but ignored.

🌐 In the Web UI

Click the ✨ Vibe button in the top control bar to open the modal dialog. Enter comma-separated seed genres, tracks or artists. The audio sliders no longer change the results.

Submitting sends a payload to /api/recommendations and renders matching songs directly into the studio grid for previewing and staging.

🤖 In the AI Agent

The agent translates text prompts like "Give me uplifting dance music like Dua Lipa" into seeds:

get_recommendations(seed_artists=["Dua_Lipa_ID"], seed_genres=["dance pop"])

1 The Core Inputs It Accepts

To generate recommendations, the tool takes up to 5 Seed Anchors:

🌱 Seed Anchors (1 to 5 total)
  • seed_tracks Track IDs; the track's lead artist becomes a seed
  • seed_artists Artist IDs; songs by their collaborators are recommended
  • seed_genres Any genre keyword (e.g. synthwave, indie pop, lo-fi, house)
🎚️ Vibe Targets (0.0 to 1.0)
  • target_energy, target_valence When no seed genres are given, these pick the genres whose estimated energy and valence are closest.
  • target_danceability, target_acousticness, target_instrumentalness, target_tempo Accepted but ignored: Spotify no longer provides audio features to Development Mode apps. The output notes when they were ignored.

2 How Vibe & Type Recommendations Work in Practice

When you prompt your AI client (like Claude Desktop or Cursor), the LLM translates your natural language into seeds or a mood preset:

⚡ Scenario A: Vibe-Based Matching
Prompt: "Give me 10 songs with a dark, energetic synthwave vibe like Blinding Lights by The Weeknd."
Tool Call Executed:
  • seed_tracks: ["Blinding_Lights_ID"]
  • seed_genres: ["synthwave"]
get_recommendations(seed_tracks=["Blinding_Lights_ID"], seed_genres=["synthwave"], limit=10)
🌙 Scenario B: Mood Matching
Prompt: "Find late-night study songs, mostly instrumental."
Tool Call Executed:
  • mood: "focus" (ambient + classical)
spotify_discover_by_mood(mood="focus", limit=20)

3 What It Does vs. What It Doesn't Do

Capability Status Explanation
Artist & Track Seeds Built-in Recommends songs by the artists the seeds share songs with (their collaborators).
Genre Seeding Built-in Searches Spotify with a genre: filter, falling back to a plain search for the genre name.
Numeric Vibe Targets Estimated Spotify no longer provides per-track audio features, so energy and valence targets are matched against estimated values per genre. Danceability, acousticness and tempo can't be applied.
Text Tag Matching LLM-Driven Doesn't search plain-text tags like #workout directly; instead, the attached LLM maps text tags (e.g. "upbeat workout") to a mood preset or genres (mood="workout", seed_genres=["edm"]).

Local Library Cache & Playlist Generators (7 Tools)

spotify_sync_library, spotify_library_stats, spotify_query_library, spotify_vibe_playlist, spotify_radio_playlist, spotify_era_playlist, spotify_time_capsule_playlist
7 MCP Tools Agent Only

spotify_sync_library saves your Liked Songs and the playlists you own to a local JSON file (~/.cache/spotify-mcp/library.json, or %LOCALAPPDATA%\spotify-mcp\library.json on Windows). Queries and stats then run without calling Spotify. The generators build vibe, artist-radio, era and time-capsule playlists, using your cached songs where that helps. They show a preview first and only create the playlist when called with create=True.

🧠 How Vibes Are Estimated

Spotify no longer gives this app audio features or artist genres. Each genre has an estimated energy and valence, and a song gets a vibe from the genre searches its artist appears in, plus words in its track, album and playlist names (a playlist called "Gym" means workout). Songs nothing can be inferred about are skipped. Spotify's genre search mostly covers mainstream Western genres, so naming your playlists by mood helps most for other scenes.

🤖 In the AI Agent

spotify_vibe_playlist(mood="workout", from_library=True, create=True)

spotify_time_capsule_playlist(year=2021)
